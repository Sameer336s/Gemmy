Gemmy 1.0 — first release (`versionCode` 1, `versionName` 1.0, package `com.gemmy.overlay`).

Gemmy is a tiny standalone floating toy overlay: a purple buddy that lives on top of your home screen and every other app. Zero dependencies, ~80 KB, no internet permission, no analytics, no ads. GPL-3.0.

Highlights
- Floats over the home screen and all apps (system overlay + foreground service).
- Idle life: bobbing, breathing, blinking, looking around, waving, hopping, spinning, yawning, and falling asleep with a little "z" after about a minute untouched.
- Tap for a squish, hearts and a speech bubble; double-tap to spin; long-press for Dance / Nap (or Wake) / Hide.
- Drag anywhere with momentum and edge bouncing; position is remembered.
- Three sizes (S / M / L), changeable while running.
- Optional start on boot.

Install: open the APK on the phone, allow install from unknown sources, then grant "Display over other apps" and tap Start floating.
